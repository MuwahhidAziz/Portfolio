# Analysis

## Layer 5, Head 1

It is observed that directional prepositions give more attention to the verb they join with noun. As can be seen that `to` attends to `went` and `towards` attends to `ran`.

Example Sentences:
- He ran very fast towards \[MASK\] but was late.
- I went to \[MASK\] but i had to delay my arriavl.

## Layer 11, Head 11

In declarative sentences, it appears that the complement attends strongly towards the subject. As can be seen that the `[MASK]` attends to `Sun` and `[MASK]` attends to `moon`.

Example Sentences:
- The Sun is \[MASK\].
- The moon is \[MASK\].
